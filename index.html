About me

      
An AI-focused virtual assistant who builds skills through real projects, and is open about what’s still in progress.

    

    

      

        
I help businesses use AI to create content, answer customers and cut repetitive work.

        
My main work is generative AI content: product and commercial ad concepts, fashion and lifestyle content for affiliate marketing, cinematic video scenes and scene-by-scene storyboards. Most of that work depends on prompt engineering, especially keeping a character’s face, body, clothing and identity consistent across images and video, and transferring motion from a reference video without losing that identity.

        
Before focusing on AI, I worked as a customer service agent in the BPO industry, handling phone, email and chat support. I still run customer service for a family food business, where I also set up an AI support assistant on Meta to handle repetitive inquiries.

        
I studied Information Technology. I didn’t finish the degree, but it gave me a working understanding of software, databases, APIs and digital systems, which helps me pick up new AI tools quickly. I’m now building hands-on experience with AI workflow automation using n8n, OpenAI, Google Sheets, Gmail and Telegram.

        
I don’t present myself as an AI engineer or automation architect. I show what I can actually build, test, troubleshoot and improve, and I’m looking for teams that value that and are willing to help me grow.
